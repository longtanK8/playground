# Background Music Directory

Place your background music file here with this name:
**background-music.mp3**

Example structure:
```
public/music/background-music.mp3
```

You can find free music from:
- Pixabay Music (https://pixabay.com/music/)
- Free Music Archive (https://freemusicarchive.org/)
- OpenGameArt (https://opengameart.org/music)

These sites provide free-to-use music for projects.
